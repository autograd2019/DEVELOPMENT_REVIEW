The headline feasibility figures (total cost $17.85m, GRV $24.40m, profit $5.51m, ROI 31.81%) have been **left unchanged** because the underlying feasibility study was not supplied for re-modelling — but several pieces of new information bear on those numbers and are flagged below.

## A. Material items requiring sponsor decision/confirmation

1. **"Independent" sales pricing vs. the loan agreement.** The business case repeatedly describes the B.E. Property pricing as *independently* prepared. The Draft Loan Agreement (clause 10 and Background D) shows the **lender is a licensed builder** and, *together with B.E Property*, holds the **first and exclusive right** to market and sell all 48 lots as **House & Land Packages**. B.E Property is therefore the financier's nominated sales partner, not an arm's-length independent valuer. The updated draft softens "independent" to "prepared by B.E Property, the project's appointed sales agent" and adds a disclosure. **Confirm how you want this characterised** — describing related-party pricing as "independent" to investors is a disclosure risk.

2. **Sales model is now House & Land Packages, not bare registered lots.** Revenue, timing and the buyer pool change materially:
   - The project realises the **land component** at B.E Property-determined market rates (borrower retains price-approval right; clause 10.2/10.9). The **builder margin on the dwellings accrues to the lender**, not to the project (clause 10.3).
   - The GRV table in the business case is built on *land* lot prices, which appears consistent with the land component — but this should be **confirmed** so investors understand the project sells land and the lender sells the houses.
   - Selling-cost allowance ($795,222) and whether any commission is payable to the lender/B.E Property on the land component is left open in clause 10.8 — **confirm**.

3. **Financing terms now known and are expensive.** The Draft Loan Agreement provides a **$1,400,000 facility at 20% p.a. pre-DA / 15% p.a. post-DA (default 24%)**, secured by a **third mortgage over Scott Purcell's West End home (25 Granville St)** and a **second mortgage over 85 River Drive**. Points to resolve:
   - $1.4m is only part of the ~$10.2m of non-land capital; the business case's **Holding Costs of $2,459,951** should be reconciled against the **actual debt stack** (senior facility + this facility). The 20% pre-DA rate accrues until the DA decision, so any DA delay is costlier than the generic "interest with a buffer" wording implies.
   - The pre-DA→post-DA step-down at 20%→15% creates a strong incentive to obtain the DA quickly; the holding-cost line should state the assumed rates and loan term.
   - **Personal/related-party security** (director's home, second mortgage behind an undisclosed first mortgage on River Drive) is a sponsor-level risk worth disclosing.

4. **Planning approval is not as "certain" as stated.** The proposal is **Code Assessable** (a genuine positive — no public notification, lower objection risk), but it **departs from two benchmarks on a performance basis**:
   - **PO4** — more than four adjoining narrow (<12.5m frontage) lots in places; and
   - **PO12 / Figure 6** — the indicative Bounty Drive–Dorset Drive public road link shown in the planning scheme is **not** provided.
   The Town Planning Report argues these are justified, but they are **performance-based arguments, not acceptable-outcome compliance.** The business case's language ("demonstrably code-compliant", "concept plan demonstrably consistent with the precinct structure plan") overstates certainty and has been moderated.

5. **Updated flood modelling risk.** Prelodgement notes (items 28–30) flag that Council's **updated flood modelling is not yet adopted** but may produce **higher present-day flood levels than the current FPL (11.25m AHD)**, and that the **northern portion has been reclassified from overland flow to medium/high flood hazard.** The Bravo Consult flood report addresses the *current* 1% AEP requirement, but Council expressly warns of the risk of "going to market with developments that are deemed flood affected at a future date." This has been added as a risk.

6. **Dorset Drive settlement vs. construction start timing.** 3-7 Dorset Drive settles **31 March 2027**, but the indicative timeline starts **civil construction in March 2027** — and Dorset Drive is the **sole approved access** for the estate. You cannot build the access road before controlling the access parcel. **Confirm** the sequence (and whether early access/works rights are negotiated into the Dorset contract).

## B. Factual corrections / status updates applied

- **DA status:** changed from "due for lodgement in May 2026" to reflect that the **DA documentation is finalised (early June 2026) for lodgement as Code Assessable RAL (3 into 48 lots on a CTS)**. Prelodgement reference DA/2026/0091.
- **Access:** now via a **new private road off Dorset Drive** (Modus "Option 3", the preferred traffic outcome), plus a **proposed 10.5m wide, ~240m access easement** benefiting Lot 22 on SP281309 (southern portion of 32 Bounty Drive). Direct access to Torrens Rd / River Drive is **not supported** by Council.
- **Internal road:** engineering documents describe a **10.5m road connection with a 6.5m carriageway**; the original "10-metre carriageway" wording was reconciled to the engineering reports (note prelodgement item 37 required a minimum 10m internal road — confirm final cross-section).
- **Stormwater / LPOD:** updated to the engineered solution — **four on-site detention (OSD) tanks with headwalls** draining to the creek (Bravo Consult), in addition to the existing drainage easement.
- **DA fees:** the DA lodgement fee is **$33,443** (base $2,928 + access easement $2,915 + $575 × 48 lots), now including the **access easement** component not in the original.
- **Consultant team named:** Town planning — Steffan Harries (Alex Steffan); Civil/stormwater/flood — Bravo Consult (RPEQ Isaac Scott); Traffic — Modus (Bradley Fuller / Harj Singh RPEQ); Landscape — Registered Landscape Architect.
- **Appendix list:** updated to the actual DA lodgement set (A–I, DA Form 1, Land Owners Consent).
- **Density narrative:** retained 23.8 lots/ha over 20,149 m² net developable; noted prelodgement context that the *earlier* 30-lot scheme over Torrens/River alone (27.77 lots/ha) was **not supported**, and the 3-parcel consolidation resolved both density and access.
